What's going on

The observer only sees your position, with noise, ten times a second. It runs the Rao-Blackwellized particle filter from the IFAC 2026 paper: 1,500 hypotheses about your goal, each assuming you're a stabilizing agent heading there, reweighted by how well it predicts your next step.

Heading straight for the ★ gives it away, because stability itself is the leak: once it knows your goal, it knows where you'll be on the way and where you'll stop. The CDC 2026 paper's counter-move is to keep the belief ambiguous while still arriving on time. Curve in rather than beelining.
